Free French, Spanish & German verb conjugation and vocabulary practice

verble is a calm, minimal trainer for learning to conjugate verbs and remember vocabulary. You see a verb, a tense and a pronoun (for example finir · futur simple · nous), type the answer (finirons) and press Enter. Right answers chime; mistakes come back a few questions later until they stick.

50 verbs and 50 words free every day, no account. Unlimited practice is €2.99 a month, or €24.99 once for lifetime access.

What you can practise

  • French: 138 verbs, including the 100 most common, including être verbs, spelling-change verbs (manger, acheter, appeler) and irregulars, in 9 tenses from présent to conditionnel passé
  • Spanish: 123 verbs, including the 100 most common, including stem-changing (dormir → durmió) and irregular verbs, in 11 tenses including both forms of the imperfect subjunctive
  • German: 125 verbs, including the 100 most common, including strong, mixed, modal and separable verbs (stehe … auf), in 6 tenses from Präsens to Konjunktiv II
  • Vocabulary: 532 everyday words in 14 topics (food, home, travel, school, work, clothes, hobbies…), translated in either direction, with articles to learn noun gender

How it works

  • Choose your tenses, pronouns, topics and an easy, medium or hard difficulty
  • Type with accent shortcuts (1–9) or tap the accent keys on a phone; lenient mode ignores accents
  • Wrong answers resurface after 3–5 questions, then again later, until you get them right twice
  • Streaks, accuracy and progress are saved in your browser

Questions

Is verble free?

Yes. You get 50 verbs and 50 words free every day, with no account. Unlimited practice is €2.99 a month or €24.99 once for lifetime access.

Which languages can I practise on verble?

French, Spanish and German. Each has its own verb trainer and vocabulary trainer, and English translations.

Which French tenses does verble cover?

Présent, imparfait, futur simple, conditionnel présent, subjonctif présent, passé composé, plus-que-parfait, futur antérieur and conditionnel passé, including être verbs with agreement (elle est allée).

Which Spanish tenses does verble cover?

Presente, pretérito indefinido, imperfecto, futuro, condicional, presente de subjuntivo, imperfecto de subjuntivo (both -ra and -se forms), pretérito perfecto, pluscuamperfecto, futuro perfecto and condicional perfecto, with stem-changing and irregular verbs.

Which German tenses does verble cover?

Präsens, Präteritum, Perfekt (with haben or sein), Plusquamperfekt, Futur I and Konjunktiv II, including strong, mixed, modal and separable verbs such as aufstehen.

How does verble help me remember?

Anything you get wrong comes back a few questions later, and again after that, until you get it right twice in a row. You can choose tenses, pronouns, topics and an easy, medium or hard difficulty.

Do I need to type accents?

Number keys 1–9 type accented letters (é, è, ç, ñ, ü, ß…), there are tap-to-type accent keys on phones, and a lenient mode accepts answers without accents.

Does verble work on my phone?

Yes. verble works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, with nothing to install.
